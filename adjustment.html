Safety Rules
Warehouse only.
Stock Adjustment corrects Warehouse physical stock. It does not edit salesman Batch Stock or damaged stock.
No negative stock.
A decrease larger than the current Warehouse physical quantity is blocked both on screen and again inside Supabase.
Cost handling.
Increase uses the current weighted average cost. Decrease automatically consumes purchased Warehouse stock first, then zero-cost Warehouse stock if needed.
